01 / The club

More than
a game.

A young club with an old soul, built on the belief that football can give a community a shared language.

Our story

Strong roots.
Brave branches.

Baobab United FC began with a simple idea: create a football home where local talent can be seen, supported, and challenged to grow. Our badge is a promise to stay connected to the people and places that made us.

We are ambitious on the pitch and generous beyond it. Every training session, matchday, and community project is another branch reaching outward.

What guides us

Our values.

01

Belonging

Everyone has a place in this club, whether they wear the shirt, support from the stand, or help behind the scenes.

02

Courage

We play forward. We make brave choices and learn from every result without losing our identity.

03

Growth

We invest in people. The best version of this club is built one player, coach, volunteer, and supporter at a time.
